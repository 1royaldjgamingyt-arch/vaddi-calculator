# Privacy Policy for Vaddi Calculator

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Application:** Vaddi Calculator (`com.vaddicalculator.app`)

---

## 1. Overview
Vaddi Calculator ("the App") is developed as an offline-first financial calculator utility. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
The App does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information. 
- No account registration or login is required.
- No user identity, email, name, phone number, or device identifiers are collected.
- No analytics trackers or telemetry libraries that gather user metrics are present.

## 3. Local Data Storage Only
All calculation history and user preferences (such as selected theme, language, and currency) are stored strictly and exclusively on the user's local device storage using Android's sandboxed SQLite / Room database. 
- No data is ever uploaded to external cloud servers.
- Users can permanently delete individual calculation records or erase all history at any time through the App's interface.
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally saved data automatically.

## 4. Android Device Permissions
The App does **NOT** require or request sensitive permissions, including:
- No Camera permission
- No Microphone or Audio recording permission
- No Location (GPS or Network) permission
- No Contacts, Calendar, or Accounts permission
- No SMS, Phone, or Call log permission
- No External Storage read/write permissions

The App operates with standard sandboxed execution privileges.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising
In its default configuration, the App does not serve third-party advertisements or use third-party tracking SDKs. Any future advertising integration will follow strict Google Play Developer Program policies and will not access user calculation inputs.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 or of any age.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official application support email or Google Play developer page.
